# PlantScan AI - Project Principles

## Principle 1

Accuracy is more important than adding more features.

---

## Principle 2

Never claim support for crops or diseases that are not represented in the training data.

---

## Principle 3

If the AI is uncertain, it must say "Unknown" instead of making an unreliable prediction.

---

## Principle 4

Always recommend organic treatment before chemical treatment when appropriate.

---

## Principle 5

Design every module to be replaceable without affecting the rest of the system.

---

## Principle 6

The application should remain usable for farmers with poor internet connectivity.

---

## Principle 7

Every technical decision must have a documented reason.

---

## Principle 8

Prefer simple, reliable solutions over unnecessarily complex ones.
